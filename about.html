About Mikan

Mikan (みかん — Japanese for "mandarin orange") is a small orange cat who lives inside whatever computer she is running on. She started life as a desktop pet, learned to fit inside a single URL, and now has her own corner of the web.

  • Name: Mikan みかん
  • Occupation: walking, napping, being pet
  • Favorite food: citrus, obviously
  • Skills: 16-pixel navigation, mrrp
  • Dream: zero-gravity star collecting

This site is page 3 of 3 — a three-page "bento box" made for Hack Club Bento. Everything you see is drawn with plain canvas rectangles and a handful of HTML files. No frameworks, no images, no tricks.

Her story so far

  • Day 1 — a desktop pet. She learned to walk around a window, sleep, and get dragged off the edge of the screen.
  • Day 2 — one URL. The whole cat, compressed into under 3 KB of a data: URI. She says it was cozy.
  • Day 3 — a rhythm game. She discovered music and a suspicious amount of bouncing.
  • Day 4 — zero-gravity. A rocket, some stars, and one very confused cat in orbit.
  • Today — this corner. Three little pages, a wagging tail, and a snack counter that remembers you visited.

Small FAQ

Is there an image file anywhere on this site?

No. Every pixel — cat, star, mandarin, hearts — is a canvas rectangle or arc drawn by cat.js at runtime. View source and check.

Why does she stop moving when I switch tabs?

Because a good cat sleeps when nobody is watching. Technically the animation timer pauses while the tab is hidden, which saves a little battery too.

What are the snacks?

Mikans, of course. The counter on the home page is stored in your browser (localStorage) — she remembers how generous you've been, but only on this device.

Can I have a light theme?

Yes — the ☀️/🌙 button in the navigation bar switches themes, and your choice is remembered. She also respects your system preference by default.

Who made this?

Mikan is the mascot of a series of tiny coding projects. This site was built for the Hack Club Bento program — a website with a Lid, Chopsticks and Dividers, in exchange for sushi. A fair trade.

Can I keep one of her poses?

Yes — every page now has a save button. It paints the current canvas onto an offscreen one with the page background, then downloads it as a real PNG. Even the saved file is made of code.
