About Mikan

Mikan (みかん — Japanese for "mandarin orange") is a small orange cat who lives inside whatever computer she is running on. She started life as a desktop pet, learned to fit inside a single URL, and now has her own corner of the web.

  • Name: Mikan みかん
  • Occupation: walking, napping, being pet
  • Favorite food: citrus, obviously
  • Skills: 16-pixel navigation, mrrp
  • Dream: zero-gravity star collecting

This site is page 3 of 3 — a three-page "bento box" made for Hack Club Bento. Everything you see is drawn with plain canvas rectangles and a handful of HTML files. No frameworks, no images, no tricks.
